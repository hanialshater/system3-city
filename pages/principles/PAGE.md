# Principles

Every company in the city follows [PRINCIPLES.md](../../PRINCIPLES.md). Breaking one needs a reason in a decision record. This page shows where the company stands: the dashboard contract's `principles`, from Meta's audit tool (`node src/principles.mjs <company dir>`). Each principle is in one of three states:
- **followed**: its record shows it;
- **gap**: the record shows it missing;
- **checked by hand**: no record can show it.

Every company copies it. Onboarding copies it by default.
