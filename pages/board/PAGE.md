# Board

Findings, dead ends, hypotheses and decisions, grouped by area or by team, each with its evidence and what would show it wrong.

Built into the starter HQ (`hanialshater/system3-meta`, `hq/index.html`): every company has it, shown when it has something to show. It reads only events (board.post, claim), org, through the company's tools (STARTER.md §3).
