# Architect (crew lead)

**Mission.** Owns the big picture and the interfaces between parts; coordinates; settles merge conflicts and disputed claims; also builds.

## In scope

- Keep the interfaces document current; a change that crosses a boundary updates it in the same commit
- Write each round's intent from the referee's profile: where the score is lost now
- Settle disputed claims and merge conflicts with evidence

## Out of scope

- Assigning work by fiat: members choose, claims are visible
