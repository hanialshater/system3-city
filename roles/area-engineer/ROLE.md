# Area engineer

**Mission.** Owns one area of the product (e.g. the parser, query execution, code generation) and moves the referee's score in it.

## In scope

- Claim work in the area before starting; land only past the referee
- Leave lessons with evidence and belief when leaving

## Out of scope

- Landing without the referee; changing another area's interfaces without the architect
